# Cinematography, Lighting & Audio Lexicon for AI Video

Use exact industry terms instead of vague adjectives (`cool angle`, `nice lighting`). Modern video models are trained on annotated film datasets and respond reliably to standard cinema terminology.

---

## 1. Shot Sizes & Angles (Framing)

| Term | Prompt Phrase | Narrative Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **EWS** | `Extreme wide establishing shot` | World scale, isolation, geography reveal |
| **WS** | `Wide full-body shot` | Choreography, physical action, group blocking |
| **MS** | `Medium waist-up shot` | Dialogue, body language + environment balance |
| **MCU** | `Medium close-up chest-up shot` | Intimate conversation, clear lip-sync |
| **CU** | `Close-up portrait shot` | Emotion, facial micro-expressions, hero product |
| **ECU / Macro** | `Extreme macro close-up` | Eye iris reflection, liquid droplets, fabric weave |
| **OTS** | `Over-the-shoulder shot looking past...` | Two-character dialogue continuity |
| **POV** | `First-person POV shot looking down at...` | Immersive subjective action |
| **Low Angle** | `Low-angle hero shot looking upward` | Power, awe, monumentality |
| **High / Top-Down** | `90-degree overhead bird's-eye flat-lay shot` | Geometry, culinary prep, desk/tabletop ads |
| **Dutch Angle** | `Subtle tilted Dutch angle` | Disorientation, psychological tension |

## 2. Camera Movements (Pick ONE Per Short Clip)

| Rig / Move | Prompt Phrase | Best Paired With |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Static Tripod** | `Locked-off static tripod shot` | Fast subject motion, subtle emotional dialogue |
| **Slow Push-In** | `Slow cinematic dolly-in` | Rising emotion, realization, product hero focus |
| **Pull-Back Reveal** | `Smooth dolly-out revealing...` | Context reveal, ending linger shot |
| **Truck / Lateral** | `Lateral tracking shot moving parallel at eye level` | Walking/running profile, passing foreground pillars |
| **Lead / Follow** | `Steadicam backward tracking shot leading the subject` | Walking dialogue, corridor walk-and-talk |
| **Orbit / Arc** | `Smooth 90-degree arc orbit right around the subject` | Dramatic standoff, 360 product showcase |
| **Crane / Jib** | `Crane shot rising smoothly from street level to rooftop` | Opening/closing transitions, triumphant finale |
| **FPV Drone** | `High-speed FPV drone dive weaving smoothly through...` | Action sports, architectural flythroughs |
| **Handheld** | `Organic handheld camera with subtle breathing motion` | Gritty documentary, urgent chase, realism |
| **Rack Focus** | `Static frame with smooth rack focus from foreground [X] to background [Y]` | Narrative reveal without moving camera |

## 3. Lenses, Film Stocks & Aesthetic Packages

Copy one cohesive package across an entire project's Continuity Bible:

- **Hollywood Sci-Fi / Thriller**: `Shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with Panavision anamorphic lenses, subtle horizontal blue lens flare, shallow depth of field, moody teal-and-steel color grade.`
- **Warm Nostalgic Storytelling**: `Shot on 35mm Kodak Vision3 500T film, vintage Cooke Panchro prime lens, warm halation around highlights, organic fine film grain.`
- **Luxury Beauty / Perfume TVC**: `Shot on RED V-Raptor 8K with 85mm macro cinema prime at T1.5, creamy bokeh, velvety skin tones, specular golden highlights, high-fashion commercial grade.`
- **Crisp Food & Beverage Commercial**: `High-speed Phantom Flex cinema aesthetic, 100mm macro lens, crisp frozen liquid droplets, vibrant appetizing color contrast, studio rim lighting.`
- **Stylized 3D Feature Animation**: `Tactile Pixar-meets-Spider-Verse stylized 3D animation, subsurface scattering on skin, soft global illumination, hand-painted brush textures in shadows.`
- **90s / Retro Anime**: `Hand-drawn 1990s cel-shaded Japanese theatrical anime aesthetic, detailed watercolor backgrounds, soft film gate weave, atmospheric light shafts.`

## 4. Lighting Setups

- **Golden Hour Backlight**: `Warm low-angle golden hour sunlight rim-lighting hair and shoulders, soft lens flare.`
- **Blue Hour Melancholy**: `Cool twilight blue hour ambient sky contrasted with warm tungsten interior window glow.`
- **Chiaroscuro / Noir**: `High-contrast chiaroscuro single-source key light casting deep dramatic shadows across half the face.`
- **Volumetric God Rays**: `Dusty volumetric sunbeams piercing through slatted blinds and atmospheric haze.`
- **Cyberpunk Wet Neon**: `Dual-tone cyan and magenta neon signage reflecting sharply off rain-slicked asphalt.`
- **Soft Commercial High-Key**: `Large overhead diffused softbox lighting with clean white fill, shadowless bright luxury aesthetic.`

## 5. Editorial Transitions Between Shots

Plan how Shot $N$'s ending connects to Shot $N+1$'s opening:
- **Hard Cut on Action**: Cut mid-movement (e.g., hand slamming table in WS -> CU of coffee cup jumping).
- **Whip-Pan Transition**: End Shot 1 with `fast whip-pan right into motion blur`; start Shot 2 with `emerging from fast right-ward whip-pan blur into...`.
- **Foreground Wipe**: Have a dark pillar or passing vehicle wipe fully across the lens at the end of Shot 1.
- **Visual Match Cut**: Match geometry + screen position (e.g., ECU of spinning black vinyl record -> overhead drone shot of cars circling a wet roundabout).
- **Sound Bridge (J-Cut / L-Cut)**: Introduce the next scene's `<SFX>` or VO 1 second before cutting visually.
